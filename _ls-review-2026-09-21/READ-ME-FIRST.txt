1. BEFORE UPLOADING: wire the Buy button
----------------------------------------
The page has a Buy button that currently points at the store's front page. To
make it real, recreate a product in Lemon Squeezy (the test one was deleted):

  Products -> New Product
    Name             Cosmic Courier (sample listing)
    Price            4.99
    Tax category     Video Game
    Licence keys     ON, activation limit 10, "Set length to unlimited"
    Publish

  Then: the product -> Share -> copy the Checkout Link

In store-demo.html, find this line (search for "buyBtn"):

  <a class="buy" id="buyBtn" href="https://retrovault.lemonsqueezy.com/">Buy — €4.99</a>

and replace the href with that checkout link. One edit, nothing else changes.

(If you would rather not have a live checkout on it, leave it as it is — the page
already explains that the listing is a sample. The reviewer mostly needs to see
the presentation and the delivery flow.)


2. UPLOAD
---------
hPanel -> Websites -> retrovault.world -> Tools -> Bestandsbeheer ->
"Toegang tot bestanden van retrovault.world", then drop store-demo.html into
public_html/ (same route as the last two uploads).

It lands at https://retrovault.world/store-demo.html
The page carries its own CSS and needs no new assets. It is marked noindex, so
Google will not list it. It is deliberately NOT in sitemap.xml.


3. THE DEMO VIDEO (question 3) — shot list, about 90 seconds
------------------------------------------------------------
Record with QuickTime (File -> New Screen Recording) or Cmd+Shift+5. No
narration needed; if you want it, say the line in brackets. Keep the mouse slow.

  0:00  demo.retrovault.world — the shelves. Scroll the Videopac shelf a little.
        ["This is Retro Vault. Around 400 catalogued releases, free to browse."]
  0:12  Click any out-of-print game -> its page -> press START -> let it play
        five seconds.
        ["Out-of-print titles play in the browser. This part is free."]
  0:30  retrovault.world/sell-your-homebrew.html — scroll past "The revenue is
        yours" and "How it works".
        ["New games are different. This is what we offer their authors."]
  0:45  retrovault.world/store-demo.html — the sample listing. Pause on the
        price and the "What you receive" table.
        ["A paid listing looks like this. One-time purchase."]
  0:55  Click Buy -> the Lemon Squeezy checkout opens. Pay with the test card
        4242 4242 4242 4242.
  1:10  The licence-key email arriving in your inbox. Show the key.
  1:20  Back in the Vault: "I have a licence key" -> paste -> the game unlocks
        and starts. Show the Download button too.
        ["The key unlocks it in the browser, and the buyer can keep the file."]

Trim the dead time, export at 1080p, and either attach it (if under ~20 MB) or
put it on YouTube as *unlisted* and paste the link. Unlisted is fine — reviewers
open links all day.

If the checkout in step 0:55 is still in test mode, say so in the email; that is
normal for a store awaiting approval and they will expect it.
