Retro Vault is a free, offline game library for the Philips Videopac G7000/G7400, Magnavox Odyssey², Commodore 64, Commodore Amiga and MS-DOS: box art, manuals, history and gameplay clips for around 400 releases, with the emulators built in. Out-of-print titles play straight away; for anything still commercially sold, you bring your own file. This shop exists for one narrow purpose: so that people still writing new games for these machines have a place to sell them, with the revenue going to them.
