## Description

```
The Videopac Odyssey Vault is an offline browser library for the Philips
Videopac — the Magnavox Odyssey² in America, the Jopac in France. 213
cartridges with their box art, the original manuals scanned page by page, a
bit of history and a gameplay clip each, and the games playable in the browser.

Filter by machine, by where a cartridge came from, or by what the game
actually is. Nine Jopac titles that never left France, sixteen Brazilian
releases under their Portuguese names, eighteen unreleased prototypes,
and seventeen homebrew games still being written for this console today.

Everything runs on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded and there's no
account.
```

## Two things to check before publishing

**The claim about homebrew.** "Still being written for this console today" is
true, but if someone asks for names: Amok and Kill the Attacking Aliens are in
the Vault, and the recent ones came from the videopac.nl forum.

**The Meinken story.** Sources differ on the spelling — Meinken and Menkin both
appear. If someone corrects you in the comments, they may well be right.
