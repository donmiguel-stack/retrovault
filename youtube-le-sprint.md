## Description

```
Le Sprint is a homebrew cartridge for the Philips Videopac G7000 - the
Magnavox Odyssey² in America - written in Intel 8048 assembly by kuoris-dev
and released at Skrolli Party 2026.

Two players, two runners, one finish line. Wait for the 3-2-1 countdown, then
waggle your joystick left and right as fast as you can: every switch of
direction is a stride. Each runner's time appears beside their lane, timed in
PAL frames, and when both are home a press of fire on each controller starts
the next race. There is no computer opponent - bring a friend.

The whole game fits in a 2 KB cartridge, and the author has published the
source, the hardware definitions and the ROM under the MIT licence.

Play it in your browser on Retro Vault, or download it there for free:
https://demo.retrovault.world/game/new_le-sprint.html

The game and its source:
https://github.com/kuoris-dev/Le-Sprint

Retro Vault is an offline browser library for the Videopac, Commodore 64,
MS-DOS and Amiga - box art, manuals, history and a playable copy for each
title: https://retrovault.world

Captured from the open-source O2EM libretro core in G7400 mode, with both
runners scripted - each one waggles at a slightly uneven, human pace, fumbles
now and then, and tires mid-race - so these are recorded races, not records.
No ROMs or BIOS images are distributed by Retro Vault; you supply your own
dumps. This one the author gives away.
```
