## Description

```
Bird Hunt is a homebrew cartridge for the Philips Videopac G7000 - the
Magnavox Odyssey² in America - written from scratch in Intel 8048 assembly
and released in September 2026 by Ahnl66, source and all.

Three birds cross the sky each round, one at a time, and you get five shots:
the five yellow cartridges in the top corner. Hit all three and you score 10
points plus 5 for every shot you did not need. Two hits is 5, one is 1, and
missing all three halves your total - which is how a run eventually ends.

This is running in G7400 mode. The cartridge looks in the BIOS for two
opcodes that only the European Plus machine has, and when it finds them it
lays its own landscape over the playfield: the tree, the hedge line, the
grass. On a plain G7000 the same game is blue sky over green grass. All of it
fits in four 2 KB banks - the game in the last one, a slice of the background
art and a copy of the loader in each of the other three.

Play it in your browser on Retro Vault, or download it there for free:
https://demo.retrovault.world/game/new_bird-hunt.html

The game and its source:
https://github.com/Ahnl66/BIRD-HUNT

Retro Vault is an offline browser library for the Videopac, Commodore 64,
MS-DOS and Amiga - box art, manuals, history and a playable copy for each
title: https://retrovault.world

Captured from the open-source O2EM libretro core, aiming scripted from the
game's own RAM - misses included, so this is a capture rather than a high
score. No ROMs or BIOS images are distributed by Retro Vault; you supply your
own dumps. This one the author gives away.
```
